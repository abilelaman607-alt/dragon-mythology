What is a Dragon?

Dragon — a mythical creature, usually a large serpent or reptile.

In some cultures it embodies evil, in others it is a symbol of wisdom and happiness.

Common Features

  • Body covered in scales
  • Wings (not always)
  • Fire breath
  • Long life and wisdom

How Myths Appeared

  1. People found bones of ancient animals
  2. Legends of giant serpents appeared
  3. Stories were passed on

The Dragon in Two Cultures

West

The dragon is an enemy defeated by a hero.

East

The dragon is a kind patron, a symbol of luck.
